Technology

In 2013, Tun joined FOURLEAF as co-founder. Started in Phuket, FOURLEAF grew into a cloud system that processed millions of orders across Southeast Asia. Restaurants and five-star hotels used it, including Centara properties in Thailand and the Maldives.

FOURLEAF

  • was part of the portfolio of AIS The StartUp, a leading startup incubator in Thailand
  • was showcased with Singtel Innov8 Sparks at Echelon 2014 in Singapore
  • represented Thailand as a finalist at the APEC Accelerator Network Summit 2014 in Taipei
  • was supported by depa with funding and business advisory

Its story appeared in Tech in Asia, Yahoo Finance, e27, Post Today and Thumbsup. Tun has also been honored to speak at events including Echelon Thailand's satellite event and Phuket Creative Tourism.

Building products from the ground up gave Tun a working understanding of the full tech stack. It also left him with a lasting fascination with technology companies and the stocks behind them.

Markets

Tun discovered Bitcoin early and bought his first in 2014, drawn as much to its economics as to its technology. In 2021 he became an active trader, designing and testing his own strategies. He has since developed them into a trading system run with AI agents he built himself. He trades across commodities, metals and technology with his own capital.

Real estate

In 2022 Tun stepped into real estate with no prior experience and learned it from the ground up, one step at a time. Starting as an agent, he has worked across rentals and the buying and selling of property, with a focus on vacation homes and pool villas in Phuket. Today he manages rental homes in Phuket, Thailand, and works with buyers looking for opportunities on the island.

Mysterious Water

Mysterious Water is Tun's independent, self-funded home base. It brings his trading systems and property work together under one long-term strategy, and it's where he shares what he builds, tests and learns along the way. The idea behind the name: move quietly, adapt to the terrain, and let time do the compounding.
