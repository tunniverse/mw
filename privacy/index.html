Your privacy

This notice explains what we collect when you contact us through mysteriouswater.com, why we collect it, and how you can see, change or delete it. It follows Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA).

01Who we are

Mysterious Water is run by Tunyalit Karnjanakul in Phuket, Thailand. He is the data controller for the personal data described here. To reach us about your data, use the contact form, or message @thetunniverse on Instagram.

02What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form:

  • Always: your name, your email address, and the topic you choose.
  • Only if you add them: your WhatsApp number or Telegram username, your Instagram, X or Facebook handle, and your message.

The site has no tracking cookies and no advertising. We use privacy-friendly visitor statistics that count visits, pages and countries without cookies and without identifying you. Like any website, our host keeps short technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the site.

03Why we use it

  • To reply to you, on the app you prefer.
  • To follow up on what you asked about, such as a property, a proposal or a press request.
  • If you share a social handle, to see that you are a real person before we reply, and to stay in touch.
  • If you tick the box to receive news, to send you occasional updates by email, such as new letters and new properties.

We use your data because you asked us to contact you (PDPA section 24(3)) and because of our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (section 24(5)). We send news by email only if you agree to it (section 19). We never sell your data and never use it for advertising.

04Who else handles it

A few trusted service providers store or carry your message for us. They may process it outside Thailand, under their own security and privacy terms:

Website hosting
Hosts this website and receives the contact form.
Email service
Delivers your message to our inbox, and sends our news emails if you asked for them.
Web fonts
Serves the typefaces this site uses.
Visitor statistics
Counts visits to the site without cookies, so we can see which pages are read.
Messaging apps
If you ask us to reply on a messaging app or social media, that service carries the conversation.

We share your data with no one else, unless the law requires it.

05How long we keep it

We keep your details while we are in touch. If you asked for news, we keep your name and email address to send occasional updates by email, and nothing else. Every news email has an unsubscribe link, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Once you do, we stop sending and remove you from the list.

Your message and any other details you sent are deleted when we no longer need them to reply to you. You can ask us to delete everything sooner, at any time.

06Your rights

Under the PDPA you can:

  • ask for a copy of the data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct it, or to delete it;
  • object to how we use it, or ask us to stop using it for a while;
  • withdraw your consent, where we rely on it;
  • complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee if you think we have handled your data wrongly.

Send your request through the contact form and we will answer within 30 days.

07Changes

If we change this notice, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
